About me

Hi I'm Craig.

By day, I lead SEO at Bounteous, managing organic strategy for enterprise brands like Best Western, Chase, KKR, and Gerber. On the side, I work with a select number of freelance clients, bringing that same level of thinking to smaller teams who want SEO done right.

I've spent 13+ years in organic search across e-commerce, hospitality, food & beverage, and local businesses. I know what works, what doesn't, and how quickly things change.

My focus is simple: build something that lasts. Not short-term spikes, but sustainable growth that compounds over time.
