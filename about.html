About me

My approach to SEO.

Three principles that shape every engagement I take on.

Focus on what actually matters.

Not everything in SEO is worth doing. I focus on the work that has a real impact on your business, not just checking boxes or chasing minor improvements.

Think like a user, not just a crawler.

Visibility follows good experiences. I think about how real people use a site, not just how search engines crawl it — visitors should find what they need quickly.

Keep things honest and transparent.

No jargon, no inflated reports, no vanity metrics. You'll always know what I'm working on, why it matters, and how it's driving results.
