About me

Hi I'm Craig.

By day, I lead SEO at Bounteous, managing organic strategy for enterprise brands like Best Western, Chase, KKR, and Gerber. On the side, I work with a select number of freelance clients, bringing that same level of thinking to smaller teams who want SEO done right.
